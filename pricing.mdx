## How to subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an API key">
    Visit the [Dashboard](https://polydata.live/dashboard) and click **Create API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click **Subscribe** on your desired plan in the dashboard, or call:
    
    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.polydata.live/v1/billing/subscribe \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer pd_live_xxxxx" \
      -d '{"plan": "monthly"}'
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay with crypto">
    You'll be redirected to a NOWPayments invoice page. Send the exact amount in your chosen cryptocurrency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto-upgrade">
    Once the payment is confirmed on-chain, your account upgrades automatically. Check status at `/v1/billing/status`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there auto-renewal?">
    No. Subscriptions are non-recurring — you pay once and get access for the period you purchased. No surprise charges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. There's nothing to cancel because there's no auto-renewal. Your access expires at the end of your paid period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I need a custom plan?">
    For enterprise pricing, dedicated infrastructure, or specialized data needs, contact us at hello@polydata.live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you offer refunds?">
    We don't offer refunds for cryptocurrency payments due to volatility, but we'll work with you on disputes via email.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
